Privacy Policy

easyapply · Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who this app is for

easyapply is a personal-use application operated by its owner for their own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, easyapply requests read-only access to your Gmail messages and your basic profile information (name and email address), solely to display your messages to you inside the app.

How the data is used

  • Email content is fetched from Gmail only to be displayed to the signed-in user.
  • The app does not send, modify, delete, or analyse your emails.
  • Email data is not stored on any server beyond what is needed to display it during your session.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

easyapply's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke easyapply's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
